Time away
from the desk.

When I have the time and energy, I head outside. Hiking, camping, traveling, and outdoor activities give me room to recharge and enjoy a different pace.

Places that stay with me.

01 · Forked Lake · Adirondacks, New York

A campsite reached
by kayak.

On a recent camping trip to Forked Lake, we loaded our gear into a kayak and paddled to our campsite. Getting there was part of what made the experience so memorable.

A little about this place +

At Forked Lake, most campsites are reached by boat or trail rather than by car. Some can only be reached from the water.

Source · New York State DEC ↗

02 · Mount Jo · Adirondacks, New York

A good day
for fall colors.

I visited Mount Jo for the fall foliage in 2026. The colors were beautiful, and it was a welcome chance to spend time on a trail and take in the view.

A little about this place +

Mount Jo was named in 1877 for Josephine Schofield. The name carries a personal story into a landscape known for its views over Heart Lake and the High Peaks.

Source · Lake Placid ↗

03 · Mount Sugarloaf · South Deerfield, Massachusetts

A view I won’t
forget.

The view from Mount Sugarloaf is one of the most beautiful scenes I’ve ever seen. It is a reminder of how much there is to enjoy close to Amherst.

04 · Sedona · Arizona

Among the
red rocks.

Sedona’s towering red rocks, pockets of green, and small pools tucked into the stone made this a beautiful place to pause. Even beneath a cloudy sky, the colors and shapes of the landscape were striking.

A change of scenery.
A little more energy for what comes next.
