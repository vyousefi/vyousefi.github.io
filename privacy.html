Privacy & analytics.

This website uses Google Analytics to help me understand visits, pages viewed, and how people find my work. Google Analytics may use cookies and collect browser and device information.

Some links I share include campaign parameters. These help me distinguish visits arriving through different application or outreach links. They indicate which link was used and do not establish who visited the website.

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. This site does not have a contact form. Email links open your email application, and external links take you to other websites with their own privacy practices.

You can disable this site’s analytics in this browser. This saves a preference on your device. The site also skips analytics when the browser exposes Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

This preference does not delete data already collected. Browser blockers and privacy preferences can also prevent visits from appearing in analytics reports.
