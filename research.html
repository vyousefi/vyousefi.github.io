Current projects

What I’m working on.

Marketing strategyDissertation

Executive cognition and market-based assets

How do leaders’ beliefs shape the assets that help firms compete? My dissertation examines how executive overconfidence influences the development of market-based assets. It aims to help firms understand the longer-term marketing consequences of leadership decisions.

Leadership & lasting firm value01
Marketing educationUnder review

Navigating academic communities in marketing

This project examines the different paths marketing scholars take through the field. It offers doctoral students and mentors a broader basis for thinking about specialization, intellectual communities, and the development of a scholarly identity.

Supporting the next generation of scholars02
Sales & employee decisionsWork in progress

A meta-analytical assessment of push-pull-mooring theory

What encourages people to leave, draws them toward alternatives, or keeps them in place? By bringing prior evidence together, this project seeks to clarify these forces and inform how organizations support and retain employees.

Understanding decisions to stay or leave03
Consumer behaviorWork in progress

Consumer resistance to subscription restrictions

Subscription restrictions can change how consumers experience products they own. This research examines consumers’ resistance to these restrictions, with implications for consumer well-being and firms’ relationships with their customers.

Teaching & community

In the classroom.

Teaching is one of the most fulfilling parts of my academic work. It is a way to serve my community by helping students develop skills they can carry into their careers and everyday decisions.

In my classes, students connect marketing ideas to practical questions. I want them to feel comfortable asking why, working with evidence, and explaining what they have learned.
